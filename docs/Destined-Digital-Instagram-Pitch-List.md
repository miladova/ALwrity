## 1. The thesis (say this first)

Destined Digital has a **distinctive voice** and **real proof assets** (`/testimonials` growth screenshots), but the profile does not surface **what it sells**, **what it costs**, or **what it achieved**. The failure is **proof and offer discoverability**, not proof existence. Fix the **permanent surfaces first** — name, links, three pins, five Highlights — then add a **low-effort publishing rhythm** that a small team can actually sustain. Confidentiality is a design constraint, not an afterthought: the marketing is done **for clients**, so every proof idea needs a clearance-free default.
