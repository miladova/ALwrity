## 1. The ideas

### Idea 1 — 3 pinned posts

Three fixed tiles at the top of the grid = the profile's landing page. Pitch it as **"the first three things a visitor sees"**, not as three random best posts.

| Slot | What the post is | Why it is there |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Who we are** — founder / team intro with a face | A stranger decides in seconds whether this is a person or a faceless agency |
| 2 | **What we offer** — services at a glance + how to start | Answers "show me the product, where can I get it" |
| 3 | **Why trust us** — a credential or a piece of proof | Removes the last doubt before a follow or DM |

**Pitch line:** "Three permanent slots that answer who / what / why-trust, instead of three posts about who we are."

---

### Idea 2 — Before & after: brainstorming → final product

A **process reveal** series. The appeal is the gap between the messy start and the polished finish — it shows *how the work happens*, which no other series does.

| Execution | Format | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| Moodboard → three rough routes → final poster | Carousel, 4–5 slides | One build, reusable template |
| First-draft caption vs the caption that shipped | 2-frame Story or a single image | ≤2 min |
| Brief → storyboard → final frame | Carousel | One build |
| "The idea we killed" + one line on why | Single image + caption | ≤5 min |
| One hour of a shoot in four phone snaps | Carousel or Story sequence | Phone only |
| "Same brief, three different directions" | Carousel | One build |

**Pitch line:** "Show the thinking, not just the deliverable — the process is the content."

⚠️ **If the final product is client work, it needs written permission.** Default to your own posts, spec/fictional-brand work, or fully owned assets. No client logo, packaging, screenshot or unreleased campaign.

---

### Idea 3 — Gen Z lifestyle / advertising memes

The **voice** series. It is the clearest expression of the locked bio line — *marketing doesn't have to be boring or repetitive* — and it is cheap to make.

| Execution | Format | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| "POV: the client says 'make it pop'" | Meme card / short Reel | 1 card |
| Marketing jargon translated into plain English | Carousel, 5 slides | One build |
| "Types of people on a shoot day" | Meme card | 1 card |
| Expectation vs reality: "one small revision" | 2-panel single image | 1 card |
| "Our camera roll vs the grid" | Carousel of phone snaps | Phone only |
| "Friday 5pm and one more change" | Native-text Story / meme | ≤2 min |
| "Gen Z office starter pack" | Meme card | 1 card |

**Pitch line:** "Be the agency that sounds like the people it is talking to — not a corporate capability deck."

⚠️ Keep memes **buyer-facing**, not just inside jokes other marketers understand. Don't let casting/model content drift into this series.

---

### Idea 4 — Aesthetic / comfy shoots and work life

The **human / BTS** series — the "most aesthetic and casual" part, in your words. People-centred content is the strongest performer on this account, and it needs almost no production.

| Execution | Format | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| 11am / 12pm / 1pm / 2pm — what we were doing each hour | 4-frame Story or photo carousel | Phone only |
| "What a shoot day actually looks like" — coffee, lights, setup, breaks | Carousel, 6 phone snaps | Phone only |
| Team working together, unposed | Single image or 5-second clip | ≤1 min |
| The comfy corner / studio details | Single image | ≤1 min |
| "Behind the effortless product shot" — resurface the 10 Aug Reel | Reel | Reuse, no new asset |
| Sunday prep for a Monday shoot | Story | ≤1 min |

**Pitch line:** "A warm, recognisable world behind the work — without a studio production."

⚠️ "Aesthetic" should be a **feel**, not a grading or design pass. Raw phone snaps read as more credible than polished ones. No client products, logos, screens or dashboards in frame; staff consent captured once.

### Idea 5 — Still thinking...

**One suggestion, since you asked me to add anything I want:**

| Option | What it is | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| **★ "The receipts" — proof / results, clearance-safe** | Own-account experiments ("we rewrote our own caption, here's what changed"), spec work for a fictional brand, aggregate counts ("12 reviews this quarter"), or a cleared case study with **one number** | It is the one thing your first four ideas do not do: show that the work actually worked. Can be done entirely with your own material |
| "Save this" checklists | 8-point profile audit, "5 things to fix before running ads", one checklist carousel a week | Cheap to build once, gets saved and shared |
| "What we actually sell" | One service per slide, who it is for, what is included, how to book | Directly answers the "where can I get it" question |

**Pick one** — if you only want five ideas, I'd take **"The receipts"**, because your other four are all about who you are and how you work; this is the only one about what you achieved.

## 2. Twenty new Reel ideas

The client asked for **"20 ideas for reels ... new ideas, not something they have yet"**. These are all **`CLIPS` / Reel type**; the topic/subject is a separate axis. Each one is mapped to one of your five ideas above, is **clearance-free (green tier)**, and is phone-only with no new asset, design pass or editing suite.

**What "new" means here:** not present in the **observed 18-post mirror sample** (§14.2 / §15.2 of the posts teardown). The mirror is incomplete and stops at 29 Sep 2026, so **verify in-app** before claiming something has never been posted. Deliberately avoided: the team introduction, account-manager day-in-life, founder faith/values Reel, "Gen Z boss" carousel, crowded-market manifesto, Sunway lecture, the RM1,500 launch-package Reel, the price-rationale podcast clip, the word-search "upgrade your socials", the "last bits of June" photo dump, "smallest tweaks", product-shot BTS, the B2B messaging-clarity Reel, the client testimonial and "industries trust us".

|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** | **"If you're new here: 3 things"** — founder to camera, three text cuts: who we help / what we do / where to start. 15 s | 1 · Pins | ≤5 min |

| **R9** | **"POV: the client says 'make it pop'"** — 5-second deadpan acted reaction | 3 · Memes | ≤5 min |
| **R10** | **"Marketing jargon → plain English"** — four quick cuts ("synergy" → "we talked") | 3 · Memes | ≤5 min |

**Format notes (secondary guidance, not Meta rules):** keep them **9–25 s**, matching the account's own observed cluster, with the **hook in the first 1–2 seconds**, captions on, one clear idea and an uploaded 1080×1920 cover ([Hootsuite — Reels for business 2026](https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-reels/), [RapidTool — hook earns the first two seconds](https://rapidtoolplus.com/blog/instagram-reels-upload-best-practices-2026/)). Recurring BTS series with a **name** build familiarity and reduce production pressure, and passive capture during a normal workday is the lowest-effort filming method ([Klap — 2026 Reel trends](https://klap.app/blog/instagram-reels-trends), [Postful — Reel trends for business](https://blog.postful.ai/instagram-reel-trends/)). November 2026 trend round-ups still put **raw, unpolished 7–15 s clips** ahead of produced ones ([New Engen](https://newengen.com/insights/instagram-trends/)) — so "aesthetic" stays a feel, not a grading pass. Niche-specific humour works when it exaggerates a truth the audience already lives with ([Gainsty](https://www.gainsty.com/blog/instagram-reel-trends)).

**Guardrails:** green tier only — no client name, logo, handle, quote, dashboard, packaging or unreleased work; own team with consent captured once; own / spec / fictional-brand work in R5–R8 and R19; no unverified statistics (the "Meta 56–70%" claim stays out); one CTA at most; no new design system or editing suite. These are a **menu, not a calendar** — the cadence target lives in posts §14.5.

## 3. Three guardrails (keep these short in the pitch)

1. **Confidentiality** — no client names, logos, handles, quotes, screenshots, dashboards, packaging or unreleased work without written permission. Ideas 2 and 4 are the ones most likely to touch client material; use own/spec work by default.
2. **Low effort** — ideas 3 and 4 should stay phone-only, unpolished, native text and one sticker; a 15-minute Monday batch can cover the week. No new design system, no shoot setup, no editing pass.
3. **Keep it buyer-facing** — memes and BTS are the fun part, but the pinned trio and idea 5 are what make a visitor act.
